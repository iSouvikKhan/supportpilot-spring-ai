# Shipping Information

## Processing time

Orders are processed within 1 to 2 business days. An order shows the status PROCESSING until it is handed to the carrier, then SHIPPED with a tracking number, and DELIVERED once the carrier confirms delivery.

## Shipping options and costs

Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days and is free on orders over $50; otherwise it costs $5.99. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs $14.99. We ship with UPS, FedEx, USPS and DHL, depending on the destination.

## Tracking an order

A tracking number is emailed when the order ships. Customers can also ask the support assistant for the carrier, tracking number and estimated delivery date of any of their orders.

## Late deliveries

If an order has not arrived 3 business days after its estimated delivery date, the customer should contact support. We will open an investigation with the carrier and, if the parcel is confirmed lost, ship a replacement or issue a refund.

## International shipping

We currently ship to the United States, Canada and the United Kingdom. International orders may be subject to import duties and taxes, which are paid by the recipient.

## Changing the address

The shipping address can be changed while an order is still PROCESSING. Once an order has shipped, the address can only be changed through the carrier.
