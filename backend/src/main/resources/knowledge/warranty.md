# Warranty

## Standard warranty

All products sold by ByteBay Electronics come with a 1-year limited warranty from the delivery date. Laptops and monitors have a 2-year limited warranty.

## What the warranty covers

The warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship under normal use. It does not cover accidental damage, liquid damage, normal wear such as battery capacity loss, or damage caused by unauthorised repairs.

## Making a warranty claim

To make a claim, the customer contacts support with the order id, a description of the problem and, if possible, a photo or video. Support will troubleshoot first. If the product is defective, we repair or replace it. If neither is possible, we refund the purchase price.

## Extended protection

ByteBay Care extends the warranty to 3 years and adds one accidental-damage claim per year. It can be added within 30 days of delivery.
