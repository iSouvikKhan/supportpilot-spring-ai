# Account and Payments

## Accepted payment methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal and Apple Pay. Payment is taken when the order is placed. We do not store full card numbers.

## Order cancellation

An order can be cancelled free of charge while it is still PROCESSING. Once an order has shipped, it cannot be cancelled; the customer can request a refund after delivery instead.

## Price adjustments

If the price of an item drops within 7 days of the order date, customers can ask support for the difference to be refunded once. Price adjustments do not apply to flash sales or clearance items.

## Account security

Customers can reset their password from the sign-in page. Support staff will never ask for a password or a full card number. To change the email address on an account, the customer must confirm the change from both the old and the new address.

## Contacting a human agent

The support assistant can hand over to a human agent at any time. Human support is available Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 18:00 Eastern Time, by chat or at support@bytebay.example.
