# Returns and Refunds Policy

ByteBay Electronics is a fictional store used for this demo.

## Refund window

Customers can request a refund within 30 days of the delivery date. Orders delivered more than 30 days ago are not eligible for a refund, but they may still be covered by the manufacturer warranty.

## Which orders can be refunded

Only delivered orders can be refunded. Orders that are still processing or in transit cannot be refunded yet; the customer can request a refund once the order has been delivered. Cancelled orders were never charged, so there is nothing to refund. An order can have only one pending refund request at a time.

## How refunds are approved

Every refund request is reviewed by a human member of the support team. The support assistant can submit a request on the customer's behalf, but it cannot approve refunds. Most requests are reviewed within 1 business day. The customer is told whether the request was approved or rejected, and why.

## Partial refunds

Customers can ask for a partial refund, for example for one item in a multi-item order. The total refunded can never be more than the amount paid for the order.

## Refund timing

After a refund is approved, the money is returned to the original payment method. Card refunds usually appear within 5 to 10 business days, depending on the bank. PayPal refunds usually appear within 3 business days.

## Returning items

For refunds on physical products, the customer receives a prepaid return label by email after approval. Items must be returned within 14 days of approval, in their original packaging with all accessories. Opened software, gift cards and personalised items cannot be returned.

## Damaged or wrong items

If an item arrives damaged or the wrong item was delivered, the customer should report it within 7 days of delivery with a photo. These cases are prioritised and the return shipping is always free.
